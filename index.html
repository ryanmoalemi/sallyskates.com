About Sally

Sally has been gliding across the ice since she was a little girl. What began as a childhood love of skating grew into a lifelong passion, and today she shares that joy with others as an ice skating instructor, helping her students build confidence, grace, and a love for the ice of their own.

Off the Ice

When she steps off the ice, Sally treasures time with her kids and family. She loves cooking, tending her garden, and heading out for walks with the dogs.
